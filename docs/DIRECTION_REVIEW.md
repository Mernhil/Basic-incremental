# Direction review (2026-11-10)

A snapshot of where Basic Incremental stands at version 0.5.1 (`main` at `72c8a75`) and what could come next, written by the direction engineer. Everything here is a proposal for the user to pick in the home chat. Nothing in this file is a rule: the rules stay in `DESIGN.md`, `UI_RULES.md` and `ASSET_RULES.md`. This file is deleted by the PR that writes the last of the chosen direction into `DESIGN.md` (Scope 1 is written in; Scope 3 still uses it).

How it was made:

- Read `CLAUDE.md`, `PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md`, `DESIGN.md` (with its changelog), `UI_RULES.md` and `ASSET_RULES.md` in full, plus the game logic and content data.
- Built a Graphify graph of the repo (graphifyy 0.9.101, already installed; 361 code files and 31 docs, 3,690 nodes, 12,096 edges, 162 communities; images left out). It stays local in `graphify-out/`.
- Ran `npm run sim -- --hours 40 --seeds 5` (671 s) and each seed alone.
- Played a new game in the dev profile `direction`, then loaded the sim player's saves at 2 h, 9 h and 16 h, with screenshots, layout probes and CPU measurements.
- Ran the `a11y-*` scripts.

Sizes: **S** is one small PR (a day of Code work or less), **M** one plan doc, **L** several plan docs or new art sets.

## 1. Where the game is going

**The vision, as the decisions read.** A desktop Brave Frontier without characters: the card is the showpiece, its weapon is what you collect and grow, and everything else exists to feed cards and cosmetics. The changelog shows three moves, all in the same direction:

1. **Simpler economy, deeper cards** (2026-11-08): plots, slots, damage lines and shields went. Strength comes only from the cards you own and how far you took them (level, stars, tier, Heirloom).
2. **A real item ladder** (2026-11-09): 11 item types, six tiers plus the Heirloom, rarity caps the tier, Mythic and Void as two sides of the top. The old art survives only as legacy skins.
3. **A classic idle pace** (2026-11-10): short two-sided fights, steady ladders, rarer summons, floors and stage stars planned, the pixel-frame shell on every screen.

The game is moving from "a prototype with many systems" to "a small number of loops that each feel good". The decisions are ahead of the content: most of what holds the game back is a decided system that is not built (floors, elements, weapon identities, tier looks), not a missing idea.

**My read.** The first 8 hours are in good shape and on target. The game then runs out of structure between 10 and 15 hours: the account caps, Ascension stops paying, fights become instant and the only goals left are Frost Core tier-ups. The next work should build the decided combat depth (floors, stars, elements, identities) before adding any new system, because that is what gives the cards something to be strong against.

## 4. Pacing and balance

Thirteen of fourteen medians sit within 25% of their target, as DESIGN §14 says. The medians hide four real problems.

**Why the misses happen.**

1. **The first 12 minutes are a fixed wait.** Every seed clears the first dungeon at exactly 12 min, and the first summon comes at the same second, because the first crystals only come from the first kill. A fresh loadout falls in 24 s and needs 39 s to kill, so the player loses about twenty fights in a row while the card levels up. The first-summon target (5–10 min) cannot be met while crystals wait for that kill.
2. **Pull luck decides the first 8 hours.** First Epic ranges from 12 min to 3.95 h and First Legendary from 12 min to 8.86 h. With 11 cards per rarity and only the fighting lead mattering, one seed starts with a Legendary lead at 12 min and another plays 4 h on Commons. The 10-pull guarantee is a Rare of any job, so it does not help the fight.
3. **One run in five stalls, and it is a circular wall.** Seed 2 never clears Dungeon 3, the Glimmer Trial, a Prismatic card or a max cosmetic in 40 h. Its best fighting card is a Legendary dagger, at Gold and level 80. It stands in front of the Glacier Colossus for 32 of the 40 hours and kills it once. To grow, the card must go from Gold to Prismatic, which costs 525 Frost Cores and 455 Frost Sigils, and those drop only from the boss it cannot kill. The four runs that progress all pulled a Mythic or Void fighting card (0.3% a pull, and the pity gives Legendary or better, which is nearly always a Legendary). So the second half of the game is gated on one rare pull, and without it the run has no way forward.
4. **The game runs out of walls.** DPS against the first dungeon grows from 530 at 15 min to 5.8 million at 40 h, while the hardest boss has 7 million HP. At the end the typical run kills the Frostspire boss in 1 s and the others instantly: 64,837 kills in 40 h. Everything tied to kills inflates with it: 2,098 to 3,930 pulls in the four runs that progress (52 to 98 an hour against a target of 12 to 15; the stalled run makes 396), the account at level 50 by 14.5 h, 28 of 29 achievements done.
5. **Ascension stops at 13.5 h.** The typical run ascends at 7.15 h, 9.57 h and 13.50 h and then never again in the remaining 26 hours, with 41 Starlight earned in total. The gain is `(points / 3e6) ^ 0.4`, so doubling Starlight needs about 5.7 times the gems, and production only grows +10% a level. The Starlight shop (seven lines up to level 25) is barely touched.
6. **"Mostly exhausted" is not reachable.** It needs a T6 weapon: a Mythic or Void card (0.3% a pull) raised through tier-ups that cost 525, then 770 Frost Cores. That is the only thing still moving after 20 h.

**The sim also differs from the real game in two ways** that matter for reading it:

- It opens every portal, including the closed Legacy Gate (`openAllPortals` defaults to true). The sim player never pulled from it in these five runs, so the numbers above are not affected, but the default should match the game.
- It plays actively every 5 seconds for 40 hours. Nobody does: there is no idle or offline profile, although offline is a core promise of the genre (§6, idea 6.10).

**Proposed balance approach** (for approval; nothing is retuned here).

- **A. Fix the opening before anything else.** Either give the first pull for free (a starting 300 Summon Crystals, or a guaranteed Rare fighting card as the first goal reward), or make the first boss beatable at about 3 minutes so the first loss streak is short. This is one data change plus a sim pass, and it moves First summon into its target.
- **B. Cut the link between kill speed and crystals.** Pick one: (1) crystals per kill only up to a kills-per-minute cap per dungeon; (2) crystals on a timer while fighting, like the first dungeon's Money wage, with a purse only on first clears and stars; (3) wait for floors and stage-star chests and move most crystals there. I recommend (2) now and (3) with floors: it holds 12 to 15 pulls an hour at every stage and survives any later power change.
- **C. Tune against bands, not medians.** Add to the sim output the spread and a "worst seed" column, raise the seed count to 20 for balance PRs, and make the target "the median within 25% and the worst seed within 2×". The stalled run then fails the check instead of hiding.
- **D. Remove the circular wall and soften pull luck for the fight.** Frost Cores and Sigils need a second source that a Gold card can reach (the Glimmer Trial tiers, or the Ember Crypt at a low rate), or the Gold to Prismatic step must not ask for Frostspire drops. The 10-pull guarantee gives a Rare or better _fighting_ card on the first 10-pull of each rarity step, or the reward track hands out a fighting card of a set rarity at levels 5 and 15. The portal odds stay as they are.
- **E. Give Ascension a second leg.** Starlight from stage stars or best floor as well as gems, so pushing dungeons pays into the prestige loop; or raise the exponent slightly and reprice the shop. This waits for floors.
- **F. Add an idle profile to the sim** (checks in every 30 minutes, 8 hours offline a day) and report both columns. Targets are for active play; the idle column is there to catch dead ends.
- **G. Sequence.** A and B are small and can ship before floors. C and F are sim work with no game change. D and E belong to the floors plan, where every number is retuned anyway.

## 5. Play-through

A new game in the `direction` dev profile, then the sim player's saves at 2 h, 9 h and 16 h loaded through Settings import. The window was 1264 × 781 at 100%.

**Dead ends and unreachable content**

- **The Frost wall.** A run whose best fighting card is a Legendary can stall at the Glacier Colossus for good: the Frost Cores and Sigils for its next tier drop only from that boss (§4, point 3).
- **Legacy skins.** The Wardrobe's Weapon skins tab lists the skins and says "Earned from the Legacy Gate", but the gate is disabled, so 27 skins and the whole tab beyond "Original" cannot be reached.
- **Shard-shop card copies.** The stall sells copies of the 11 beta cards. Eight of them are Rare or better, whose stars are free, so a bought copy becomes shards at once: Dawnblade Regent costs 40 shards and pays back 8. Only the three Common tiles do anything. The other 66 cards are not sold at all.
- **Enhancements on low rarities.** Enhancements unlock at Silver ★1, but Common cards cap at Wood and Uncommon at Bronze. Their detail panel still leads with a gold Enhancements button that can never do anything.
- **Wearing cosmetics.** A card wears a border from Gold ★1 and a holo from Prismatic ★1. Common, Uncommon and Rare cards can never reach Gold, so 33 of the 77 cards can never show a border, and only the 33 Legendary, Mythic and Void cards can ever show a holo.
- **Harvest crits.** The stat exists and nothing grants a chance.
- **Starlight shop.** Seven lines with 104 levels in total; the typical run buys about ten of them in 40 h.
- **Account tree.** 23 nodes, 49 points by level 50; the sim player maxes nine nodes and never takes the Automation branch.
- **Dev cheats.** No panel exists, so a dev build cannot jump ahead without importing a save.

**Placeholders**

- Every dungeon uses the same brick room.
- Wood cards wear the Bronze frame; Mythic, Void and Heirloom cards the Prismatic frame.
- T4 Gold weapons show interim art, and the shard shop shows the old twin blades for Storm Duelist.
- Wardrobe item previews and grid thumbnails for borders are near-empty dark cards.
- The Weapon skins tab carries a "Test" badge, and the Rare scythe is still called the Reaper Test Card.
- The Details panel and the weapon price chips show coins below 1x (the known Small art rule).

**Flat spots**

- 0 to 12 min: nothing to do but lose fights and buy Oak and Copper levels.
- About 2 h onward: Dungeon 2 dies in 3 s (18 kills a minute at 2 h), so the arena is a blur and the "15 to 30 s" fight is gone.
- 14 h onward: account level capped, no further Ascension, goals strip shows only mastery tiers.
- Production after the third kind unlocks: no new decision, only the next level.
- Rare and better cards need five "Add star" clicks that cost nothing after every tier-up.

**Bugs**

- The shard hint reads "A duplicate of a card at Prismatic ★6 turns into shards": wrong since the star bar change (any copy past the bar, and every Rare+ copy, becomes shards).
- Card names are cut on almost every card at 1x ("Ember Sq…", "Apprentic…", "Silver Exe…"), and on the Cards grid the level badge covers the start of the name. Item names on the bottom plate are cut too ("Prismatic Kal…").
- Wardrobe slot plates cut their item names ("Knight …", "Clean T…", "Plum Pi…") although the plate has room for two lines.
- Shop and Achievements show a page title and scroll the whole page; the other eight pages hide the title and fit the window.
- After the PC sleeps with the game open, the live loop replays up to 8 h in 1-second steps in one go: about 28,800 steps, a freeze of many seconds, and it ignores an offline cap raised above 8 h.
- `scripts/a11y-audit.mjs` fails (it selects the removed Exotic rarity); `scripts/a11y-shell.mjs` passes but checks four selectors that no longer exist (`.hm-stage__*`, `.sk-detail__rank`); `scripts/demo-progression.mjs` still looks for a card `evolution` field.
- The sim prints the First summon target as "0.125-0.16666666666666666 h".
- The XP readout mixes formats ("274,254 / 3.35e6 XP").
- "Long Night" in the Starlight shop reads "+0 → +1" with no unit (hours).

**Measured cost** (9 h save, share of one CPU core over 5 s): Cards 55%, Production 39%, Home 26%, Dungeons 23%, Wardrobe 16%, the other pages 9 to 10%. The game logic itself is cheap (0.36 ms for a 100 ms tick); the cost is the renderer re-rendering the visible page ten times a second.

## 7. UI and art direction

### Where it is heading

The picks all point one way: **a chunky pixel desk, dark and warm, with the card as the brightest thing on it.** Four rounds of mockups ended at the pixel-frame shell (notched frames, gold or plum buttons, Pixelify Sans only for big text). **Graphify recommendation.** The graph was useful once, for orientation: it showed the hubs (`GameState`, `Content`, `collectModifiers`, `startGame`), 20 import cycles that all pass through `modifiers.ts`, and which tests cover which module. For day-to-day work in this repo it adds little over `PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md` plus search: the code is 25,000 lines with a clean folder contract, and the overview is kept equal to the code by CI. The always-on install would add a second "read this first" source to `CLAUDE.md` and a hook on every search, and a doc graph costs model time to keep fresh. Keep it installed, rebuild it on demand before a large refactor (floors), and do not turn on the always-on mode.
