Weapon panel: 5 options

Where the main card's level and XP, the tier-up (cost, max-level state) and the star bar live, once the damage lines are gone (DESIGN.md §5). Every option is shown in the two places the user asked for: the dungeon drawer (it takes the damage column) and the Cards page detail. Use the state chips to flip every mock between mid-level, max level with the tier-up affordable or not, the Frost Sigil step, a full star bar, a Unique available and a no-Unique weapon.

Pick hints

  • Want the drawer to stay light and fast to scan? A (slim strip) or E (forge popover).
  • Want the next tier visible before you can pay for it, with every cost in plain sight? B (weapon sheet).
  • Want one job per view and no wall of numbers? C (tabs), with the cost of one extra click.
  • Want it to feel like the game, not a form? D (on the weapon): gauge, level badge and pips sit on the art.
  • Want to preview the new weapon look (the interim rarity ladder, then the Unique)? E shows before and after; B only shows the ladder as dots.
  • Safest to build and test: A or B (static, no hidden state). D needs the most art work; E needs a popover that closes on Escape and outside click.
  • Whichever you pick, the Cards page and the drawer use the same component, so the choice is made once.

Notes on the numbers. Names, tiers, level caps (20 / 40 / 60 / 80 / 100), the XP curve (10 × 1.12 per level, `cardRules.json`), the star table (§5), star impact (x1.25 to x3.5), tier power (x1 / 1.5 / 2.25 / 3.5) and the tier-up materials come from the data. The Money price of each tier-up is new in §5 and not in the data yet, and so is any cost for the Unique tier: both are placeholders here. Weapon pictures are the real baked sprites on the interim rarity ladder (§5 Weapon looks): Ember Blade is Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary and then Flameblade; Bulwark starts Rare and tops out at Mythic.
