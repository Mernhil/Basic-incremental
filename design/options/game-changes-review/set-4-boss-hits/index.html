Boss hits: 5 options

Every boss hits lightly at its rhythm and every sixth attack is a telegraphed heavy slam: five light hits at 0.4x and one slam at 4x (DESIGN.md §6), so the damage per minute stays what dungeons.json says. Each option below combines three choices: how the slam is telegraphed on screen, how the health bar shows the chunk it takes (the pale trailing segment is in every one, in a different form), and where "You fall in" sits next to time-to-kill. All five stages run the same fight on one clock, so you can compare timing by scrolling. Player hits are left out on purpose to keep the boss's rhythm readable.

Pick hints

  • Want the wind-up to be about the boss? A (boss charges): it reads at a glance and costs no extra UI, but you must be looking at the boss, not at your cards.
  • Want to see where and how much it will hurt? B (ground marker) points at the party, D (bar pre-flash) shows the exact chunk on the health bar.
  • Want it to work when you are looking elsewhere (cards, drawer, Home mini-stage)? C (screen edge pulse). It is the loudest and the one most likely to annoy on a long idle session.
  • Want players to learn the pattern (5 light, then a slam)? E (countdown ring) shows the count the whole time, not only the last second.
  • Want the readout to say "this fight cannot be won" without shouting? C (banner only when red) is quietest; A (extra tile) and E (stacked in the tile) are always on; B (race bars) explains the gap; D (clock under the bar) sits next to the health it measures.
  • Reduced motion: all five drop shake, pulse, lunge and falling chips; the wind-up becomes a static marker or a still glow, and the chunk simply drops with a short fade. The readout is text and does not change.
  • Wind-up lasts 1.5 s in the real game (the 6th attack is announced by the report, DESIGN.md Phase 3); the demo pace is faster so you do not wait 36 s per slam. Switch to Real pace to feel the real timing.
