Cards on screen: 5 options

DESIGN.md section 11 (Sharp cards) and section 12 (Clean pixels): the collection gets its own compact card, the detail card gets a size, and card text is real text. The card is one 132 x 186 pixel grid and is only ever drawn at whole scales (1x, 2x, 3x) with nearest-neighbour; the frame, the item and the body are the real baked sprites and rarity colours, and the card text is the real text of the 12 beta cards (cards.json). The collection state (tiers, stars, levels) is invented. Click a card to select or open it.

Pick hint

  • A Full card, 1xPick it if the card is the thing and you want it the same everywhere, only bigger when there is room.
  • B Half-grid miniPick it if you want the whole collection at a glance and a big reveal when you open a card.
  • C List rowsPick it if you manage the collection (levels, effects, cost) more than you admire it.
  • D Window cardPick it if you want a fast item-first grid and the full card kept for the detail.
  • E 2x showcasePick it if you want the card to be the showpiece on a big screen and text to be roomy.
