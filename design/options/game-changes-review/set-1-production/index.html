Production screen: 5 options

One row per tree kind or mine type, with its tile, level, output, bar to the next x2 milestone and the buy button on the row (DESIGN.md section 4). One x1 / x10 / x100 / Max toggle. Locked kinds are greyed rows with their unlock price. The five differ in layout, density, where the production card stands, where the upgrade shelf sits and how a strike moves. Numbers are the real data (trees.json, mines.json, upgrades.json) at an invented mid-game save; sprites are the real baked tiles at whole-number scales. Click the toggle and the tabs: the mocks are live.

Pick hint

  • A LedgerPick it if you want the card to feel like the boss of each job and the page to read top to bottom.
  • B Two-upPick it if you want every number on one screen with no scrolling and the card kept small.
  • C Side stagePick it if you want the card big and alive while you shop, and the shelf out of the way.
  • D Big tabsPick it if you want few things on screen at once, large growing tiles and the card always visible.
  • E Tile boardPick it if you want it to feel like a field you tend, and are fine bending "one row per kind" into tiles.
