Pixel picks: Deck and Table, Market Stalls

Your two chosen layouts redone with real pixel art made in PixelLab (each screen five treatments that differ in palette, material, detail, shading and ornament). Nothing here is in the game. Pixel art is shown at 1 CSS pixel per art pixel in the 1x shots, and the 2x shots are the game-scale pass (every art pixel 2 device pixels); the half-resolution options (S4, H4) draw on a half-size grid and are shown at 2x on purpose. The screen thumbnails below are shrunk to fit this page, so click one for the full-size page; the art kits and GIFs are shown unscaled.

Same layouts and motion ideas as Summon 5 and Shop 2, with two changes made to keep pixels whole: the card hand is a straight arc of cards instead of a rotated fan, and the three shop stalls are an equal 320 px wide so the awnings are not stretched. Card faces and the Starlight coin are the existing game art at the mockup scale; the mini cards in the Shard shop are still the placeholder 0.5 scale and need a whole-scale card for the real screen (ui request). PixelLab credits: 1183.70 before, 858.70 after (325 generations drawn; my own calls come to about 170, the rest ran on the same account from another session).
